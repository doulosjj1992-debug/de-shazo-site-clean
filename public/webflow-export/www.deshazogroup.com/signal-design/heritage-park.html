TEXAS WOMAN'S UNIVERSITY

TRAFFIC SIGNAL AND DESIGN

Project Overview

DeShazo was retained in 2023 for designing and adding standard sheets for a pedestrian scramble diagonal crosswalk system as part of the Texas Woman’s University (TWU) Signal Design project at Brackenridge Hall in Denton, Texas. We developed construction plan sheets for traffic signal poles, detectors, conduit runs, wiring, and signal head types and locations. In addition, we prepared cost estimates for the traffic signal construction and inserted appropriate specifications for the diagonal crosswalk sheet.

📍Location: Denton, Texas – N Bell Avenue at Administration Drive
📑Scope: Traffic signal design, roadway safety improvements, and intersection planning
👤 Client: Texas Woman’s University

🚦 Signal & Traffic Engineering Plan

1. Designed a signal system to improve safety and reduce vehicle delays.
2. Integrated pedestrian-friendly crossings with ADA-compliant access.
3. Coordinated timing plans to optimize traffic circulation during peak and off-peak hours.
4. Planned for future growth and traffic demand to ensure sustainable mobility.

📌 Functional Signal Design

1. Installed modern traffic signal equipment with upgraded controllers and visibility improvements.
2. Designed intersection layouts with clear signage and lane designations.
3. Coordinated construction and phasing plans to minimize disruption during implementation.
