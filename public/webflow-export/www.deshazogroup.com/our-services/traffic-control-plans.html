Traffic Engineering Services

Traffic Control Plans (TCPs)

45 Years of Proven Experience

DeShazo Group has prepared hundreds of Traffic Control Plans for construction projects, school zones, roadway maintenance, special events, and utility work across Texas. Our plans balance safety, efficiency, and practicality so traffic flows while workers and pedestrians remain protected.

TxDOT & MUTCD aligned Constructability-ready Urban & suburban corridors

What We Do

  1. Temporary Traffic Management — lane closures, detours, and work-zone phasing that maintain flow and safety.
  2. Signage & Pavement Markings — placement of warning signs, cones, barricades, and striping for clear guidance.
  3. Pedestrian & Bicycle Safety — safe detours and crossings for non-motorized users.
  4. Regulatory Compliance — plans that meet MUTCD, TxDOT, and local agency standards.
  5. Coordination & Permitting — support for city/county approvals and contractor implementation.
  6. Event-Specific TCPs — school drop-off/pick-up, special events, and maintenance of traffic (MOT).

Why It Matters

  • Protects workers and the public in temporary or changing conditions.
  • Maintains mobility by reducing delays and congestion during construction or events.
  • Ensures compliance with federal, state, and local regulations.
  • Clarifies responsibilities for contractors, law enforcement, and road users.

Recent work includes corridor maintenance TCPs, school zone re-striping operations, and festival/event MOT across the Dallas–Fort Worth region.

Examples Projects

  • Construction and maintenance zones (arterials & collectors)
  • School campuses and drop-off / pick-up areas
  • Special events and festivals (downtown districts)
  • Roadway closures and detours for utility/telecom work
  • Bridge/overpass work with staged lane shifts
