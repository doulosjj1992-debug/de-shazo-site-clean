Complete Street Planning

Modern communities require streets that safely accommodate all users—including motorists, pedestrians, cyclists, and transit riders. DeShazo Group delivers Complete Street Planning that balances mobility, safety, accessibility, and placemaking for sustainable, livable corridors.

45 Years of Proven Experience

We’ve planned and designed complete street solutions across North Texas for urban corridors, school zones, mixed-use districts, and residential neighborhoods—aligning traffic operations with walkability, bike access, and transit.

What We Do

  • Multimodal Assessment — evaluate existing streets for vehicles, pedestrians, bicycle & transit.
  • Design Recommendations — lane configs, sidewalks, protected bike lanes, crosswalks, transit facilities.
  • Traffic Operations Integration — signals, timing, signage & intersection upgrades for efficient mobility.
  • Safety Enhancements — lighting, visibility, speed management, conflict reduction at key nodes.
  • Land Use Coordination — align with adjacent development, parks, schools, and destinations.
  • Policy & Standards — ensure compliance (ADA, MUTCD, local/state guidelines).

Why It Matters

  • Safety for all users—pedestrians, cyclists, transit riders, and drivers.
  • Less car-only dependency; more sustainable, connected mobility.
  • Accessible, attractive streets that support neighborhood vitality.
  • Integration with development and transit for long-term growth.

Park-Adjacency & Crossings

Design around destination edges (parks, museums) with safer crossings and slower operating speeds.

Walkable Districts

Calm traffic in shopping & dining corridors to prioritize crossing frequency and pedestrian comfort.

Curb & Multimodal Operations

Balance curbside loading, micromobility, and transit to keep people moving without conflicts.

Ready to upgrade a corridor or district?

We’ll align traffic operations with pedestrian, bicycle, and transit goals—practical, code-compliant, and buildable.
