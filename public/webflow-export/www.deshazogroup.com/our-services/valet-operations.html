Valet Operations

Efficient valet operations enhance guest experience, streamline site circulation, and maximize parking capacity. DeShazo Group delivers comprehensive Valet Operations Planning for hotels, restaurants, event venues, mixed-use sites, and airports—balancing safety, mobility, and convenience.

45 Years of Proven Experience

We prepare review-ready plans across Dallas–Fort Worth, coordinating with city staff and property teams to deliver practical, buildable curbside solutions.

What We Do

Market Research & Analysis

  • Site assessment & layout — locate drop-off, pick-up, staging, and egress to reduce conflict.
  • Operational flow design — intake, ticketing, staging, retrieval, and key-control procedures.
  • Staffing & resourcing — peak-period staffing, training, comms, and wayfinding.
  • Traffic integration — internal circulation, sidewalks, and adjacent roadways.
  • Safety & accessibility — ADA paths, sight distance, signing/marking, lighting.
  • Event overlays — high-demand playbooks for arenas, hotels, and special events.
