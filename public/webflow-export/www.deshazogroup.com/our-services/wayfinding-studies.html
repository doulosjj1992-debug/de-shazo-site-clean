Wayfinding Studies

Make complex places effortless to navigate.

DeShazo considers the full user journey—by vehicle, bicycle, mobility device, or on foot—when developing wayfinding for universities, medical centers, corporate campuses, and mixed-use districts. Our goal is simple: move every user from the periphery to the front door as clearly and intuitively as possible.

Effective systems coordinate the inside and outside of a site—circulation, entries, parking, and signage— so people can orient quickly and make confident decisions. We design intuitive, ADA-aware solutions that reduce conflicts and get people where they need to go, faster.

What we do

  1. Context & user audits: study desire lines, confusion points, curb activity, queues, and key decision nodes.
  2. Mode-inclusive circulation: logical routing for cars, service/emergency, pedestrians, bikes, shuttles, and deliveries.
  3. Naming & mental models: zone/entrance/level naming that scales and matches how people think about the place.
  4. Sign family & standards: hierarchy, typography, pictograms, and color for vehicular and pedestrian needs.
  5. Parking & access guidance: connect lots/garages to the right entrances; minimize search time from edge to entry.
  6. Integration & implementation: align with TMP/TIA, ADA, and agency standards; provide bid-ready sign schedules.

Why it matters

  • Safety: fewer sudden maneuvers and conflicts at key decision points.
  • User experience: intuitive, low-stress navigation for visitors, patients, students, and staff.
  • Accessibility & equity: ADA-aware solutions that serve all users and modes, including mobility devices.
  • Efficiency: shorter search time in lots/garages; smoother entries, drop-offs, and exits.
  • Value: professional, brand-consistent environments that support operations and growth.
