DeShazo Group Celebrates 45 Years of Service

45 Years of Excellence in Mobility & Planning

1. Founded in 1979 – DeShazo Group began as a specialized traffic engineering firm in Dallas, Texas.

2. Expanding with Purpose – Over four decades, we've grown into a trusted leader in transportation planning, parking analysis, and mobility consulting across the region.

3. Client-Centered Focus – From city governments to private developers, our commitment has always been delivering clear, data-driven solutions that shape better communities.

4. Enduring Values – Built on integrity, technical rigor, and public-minded service, our values continue to guide every project.

Signature Projects & Lasting Impact

1. Public Infrastructure Leadership – Supported mobility and parking planning for major projects like Klyde Warren Park, Dallas Love Field, and numerous citywide zoning reforms.

2. Trusted by Texas Cities – Partnered with over 100 municipalities across the state to develop traffic studies, thoroughfare plans, and transportation master plans.

3. Innovation in Parking Strategy – Helped pioneer performance-based parking, shared parking models, and context-sensitive demand analysis.

4. Event & Venue Expertise – Delivered operations plans for large-scale venues including AT&T Stadium, Texas Live!, and multiple university campuses.

Looking Ahead: Smarter, Sustainable Cities

1. Smart City Integration – Advancing sensor-driven planning, curb management, and data analytics for next-generation mobility systems.

2. Equity & Access Focus – Supporting projects that improve transportation access for all users, including pedestrians, cyclists, and transit riders.

3. Sustainability at the Core – Aligning transportation strategies with climate goals, land-use efficiency, and long-term resilience.

4. Commitment to the Future – As we celebrate 45 years, our focus remains clear: delivering thoughtful, practical solutions that move cities forward.
