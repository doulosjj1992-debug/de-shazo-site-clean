CHRIST CHAPEL BIBLE CHURCH

Parking Design

Project

Project Overview

DeShazo Group provided transportation planning and traffic engineering support for a phased expansion of Christ Chapel Bible Church, helping accommodate increasing attendance while reducing congestion and enhancing on-campus and surrounding neighborhood traffic flow.

⛪ Religious Facilities | 📍 Fort Worth, Texas‍‍
📐 Scope: Parking, traffic, and phasing analysis for a two-phase sanctuary expansion
🏢 Client: Christ Chapel Bible Church

🚗 Phased Expansion Analysis

1. Evaluated traffic and parking needs for planned 1,000- and 1,500-seat sanctuaries
2. Aligned parking demand with phased construction timeline to ensure adequate capacity at each stage

🅿️ Optimized Parking Strategy

1. Assessed current parking inventory and advised on placement of additional lots for best ingress/egress
2. Developed parking strategies to eliminate reliance on surrounding residential streets

Strategic Highlights

🙏 Growth with Grace, Designed with Purpose
Helping Christ Chapel expand its reach while minimizing its footprint on local traffic.

📋
Service Scheduling Optimization

1. Recommended service times and vehicle flow timing to reduce congestion at key intersections
2. Minimized overlap of peak pedestrian and vehicle activity

🚸
Pedestrian & Drop-off Improvements

1. Evaluated drop-off/pick-up zones and pedestrian routes to improve safety
2. Reduced pedestrian and vehicular conflict points near Montgomery Street and Northwest Highway
