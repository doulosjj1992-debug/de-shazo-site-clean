Frequently Asked Questions

What services does DeShazo Group provide?

We specialize in traffic engineering, transportation planning, parking analysis, and functional parking design. Our team assists both public and private clients throughout the planning, design, and permitting phases of development projects.

Do you work on both public and private sector projects?

Yes. We work with municipalities, school districts, architects, developers, and engineering teams on projects ranging from small site assessments to regional transportation studies.

Can DeShazo help with city or TxDOT permitting?

Absolutely. Our team has extensive experience working with the City of Dallas, TxDOT, and other jurisdictions to assist clients in navigating approval processes related to traffic impact analyses, access, parking, and circulation.
