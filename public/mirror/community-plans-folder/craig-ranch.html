Craig Ranch Development - Traffic Impact Study

Craig Ranch Development

Comprehensive Traffic Impact Analysis & Infrastructure Planning

Project Overview

Craig Ranch is a 140-acre residential community at Custer Road and Collin/McKinney Parkway in McKinney, Texas. DeShazo Group evaluated the site's projected traffic impact, recommending roadway enhancements to accommodate growth while preserving safety and flow. The study ensures the development complements the region's long-term mobility goals and infrastructure capacity.

📍

Location

Intersection of Custer Road & Collin Road/McKinney Parkway, McKinney, TX

🏘️

Development Size

140+ acres of residential land use

🏠

Residential Mix

208 Townhomes, 410 Single-Family Homes, 200 Multifamily Units

Strategic Impact & Recommendations

DeShazo's study guided decisions on roadway improvements to accommodate the development's traffic while minimizing congestion

⚠️ Targeted Roadway Improvements

  • Add right-turn lanes at key intersections to improve flow and reduce delays
  • Evaluate future signalization needs based on real-world traffic volumes

🚗 Projected Roadway Impacts

  • 🔴 Custer Road
  • 🟢 Collin Road/McKinney Parkway
  • 🟫 Exchange Parkway

🌐 Regional Integration & Planning

  • Coordinate with city and regional transportation authorities
  • Align improvements with broader mobility and infrastructure goals

🛣️ Regional Freeway Access

  • 🟨 SH-121/Custer Road Interchange
  • ⚠️ SH-121/Exchange Parkway Interchange
  • ✅ All other roadways in the vicinity are projected to operate below capacity during peak periods

Key Recommendations

📊 Traffic Management Strategy

Implementation of smart traffic signal coordination and adaptive signal control technology to optimize traffic flow during peak hours and special events.

🚦 Infrastructure Enhancements

Strategic placement of turn lanes, improved intersection geometry, and enhanced pedestrian crossings to ensure safety and efficiency for all users.

🌱 Sustainable Growth Planning

Long-term mobility solutions that support the community's growth while maintaining quality of life and environmental sustainability.
