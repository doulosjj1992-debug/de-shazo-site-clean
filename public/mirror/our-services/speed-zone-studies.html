SPEED ZONE STUDIES

We establish safe, defensible, and community-minded speed limits using spot speed data, roadway geometry, and crash history—aligned with TxDOT, the Texas Administrative Code, and the MUTCD.

45 Years of Proven Experience

DeShazo Group has completed speed zone studies for cities, counties, school zones, and rapidly developing corridors across Texas. We deliver clear, data-backed recommendations that withstand public review and regulatory scrutiny.

Our approach balances safety, mobility, and community needs—so projects move forward with confidence.

Market Research & Analysis

What We Do

  • 1Field Data Collection — spot speed runs, roadway geometry, adjacent land uses, and operating conditions.
  • 285th Percentile Analysis — industry-standard statistical methods to set reasonable, enforceable limits.
  • 3Safety Review — crash histories and context to identify locations needing speed management.
  • 4Regulatory Compliance — alignment with TxDOT guidance, Texas Administrative Code, and the MUTCD.
  • 5Agency & Public Coordination — collaboration with cities, school districts, and law enforcement to build support.

Community Outcomes

Why It Matters

  • 4Public Confidence from clear documentation and graphics that explain the “why.”
