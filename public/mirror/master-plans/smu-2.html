SOUTHERN METHODIST UNIVERSITY RESIDENTIAL COMMONS

TRAFFIC, TRANSPORTATION, & PARKING CONSULTING SERVICES

Project

Project Overview

DeShazo Group played a critical role in the planning of the 1,250-bed Residential Commons on SMU’s campus, a major student life initiative designed to enhance campus living. Working in collaboration with the university and its design team, DeShazo addressed transportation needs for this high-density development located on a constrained site in the heart of Dallas.

🎓 Higher Education | 📍 Dallas, Texas
‍📈 Scope: Traffic, transportation, and parking planning for new student housing master plan
🏫 Client: Southern Methodist University (SMU)

🚗 Parking Garage Planning & Design

1. Designed an 800+ space parking garage to serve both the Residential Commons and broader campus community
2. Integrated circulation and access needs into the overall site plan for optimal flow and functionality

🛣️ Roadway Network Reconfiguration

1. Reconfigured surrounding streets to create a unified and contiguous development parcel
2. Ensured smooth integration with the existing campus street grid and minimized disruptions

Strategic Highlights

🚶♂️ Walk Smart, Park Smart

✅
🔁 Campus Mobility & Access Evaluation

1. Assessed the impact of increased residential density on both pedestrian and vehicular traffic
2. Optimized safety measures and multimodal access to support students, staff, and visitors
‍

🔄
📊 Master Plan Alignment

1. Aligned traffic, parking, and circulation strategies with SMU’s long-term growth and sustainability goals
2. Supported campus objectives for walkability and efficient land use
