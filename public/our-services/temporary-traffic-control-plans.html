PROVEN EXPERIENCE

45 Years of Traffic Excellence

Since 1979, we've been at the forefront of traffic engineering and urban mobility planning. Our team of certified professionals has successfully delivered over 2,000 projects across North America, from small-town intersections to major metropolitan transportation networks.

We combine decades of field expertise with cutting-edge simulation technology to create solutions that stand the test of time. Our approach integrates traditional engineering principles with modern data analytics and AI-powered optimization.

WHAT WE DO

Comprehensive Traffic Solutions

We provide end-to-end traffic consulting services that address every aspect of urban mobility:

  • Traffic Impact Analysis & Environmental Assessments
  • Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) Design
  • Signal Timing Optimization & Coordination
  • Pedestrian & Bicycle Infrastructure Planning
  • Parking Studies & Management Strategies
  • Road Safety Audits & Crash Analysis

Each project receives personalized attention from our senior engineers, ensuring solutions that are both innovative and practical.

WHY IT MATTERS

Building Safer, Smarter Communities

Every second counts when it comes to traffic flow. Poor traffic management costs the economy billions annually in lost productivity, increased emissions, and preventable accidents.

Our data shows that optimized traffic systems can reduce:

  • Travel times by up to 25%
  • Accident rates by 40%
  • Vehicle emissions by 20%
  • Emergency response times by 30%

We're not just managing traffic – we're enhancing quality of life, one intersection at a time.
