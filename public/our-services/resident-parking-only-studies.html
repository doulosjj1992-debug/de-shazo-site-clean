Residential Parking Only Studies

We design data-driven programs that keep neighborhoods livable: balanced curb access, permit policies, and equitable on-street supply near homes, schools, and mixed-use areas across North Texas.

45 Years of Proven Experience

DeShazo Group has delivered residential and permit parking studies for Dallas-area neighborhoods, campuses, entertainment districts, and event venues—aligning community goals with operations and enforcement.

What We Do

  • Curb Inventory & Occupancy — time-of-day & day-of-week counts, supply vs. demand.
  • Permit Programs — eligibility, limits, fees, and guest/contractor passes.
  • Policy & Enforcement — signage plans, hours, grace windows, data-led enforcement.
  • Event & School Overlays — buffers and temporary controls near high-demand generators.
  • ADA & Access — driveways, loading, and accessible curb management.
  • Stakeholder Engagement — workshops, surveys, and council presentations.

Why It Matters

  • Protects neighborhood access for residents, guests, and services.
  • Reduces spillover from schools, venues, and entertainment districts.
  • Improves safety at driveways, corners, fire lanes, and crosswalks.
  • Supports ADA & equity through accessible and fair curb use.
  • Enables enforcement with clear, data-backed rules and signage.

Example Project Types

Private & Public Sector Experience
  • Neighborhood permit districts near schools & campuses
  • Event overlay plans near venues & entertainment areas
  • Mixed-use residential/retail curb allocation & loading
  • ADA & access at driveways, alleys, and corners
  • Program launch: signage, communications, enforcement
