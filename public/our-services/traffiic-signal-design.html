What we do

  1. Warrant analysis: confirm need using MUTCD tests and site data.
  2. Signal layout & plans: mast arms, poles, wiring, detection, controllers, and cabinet details.
  3. Timing & phasing: optimize phases, clearance, pedestrian timing, and coordination.
  4. Traffic control devices: integrate signing/marking for complete intersection performance.
  5. Technology: adaptive control, TSP, emergency preemption, ATSPM readiness.
  6. Agency coordination: work with cities, counties, and TxDOT for approvals and standards.

Why it matters

  • Improves safety by reducing crashes and pedestrian conflicts.
  • Reduces delays through optimized timing and phasing.
  • Supports growth with scalable hardware and plans.
  • Enables smart ops with TSP, preemption, and analytics.

Example Projects

  1. Urban intersections with high vehicle and pedestrian demand.
  2. School zones and pedestrian-heavy crossings.
  3. Retail & commercial access points with new signals and turn bays.
  4. Arterial corridors requiring progression and coordination.
  5. New development driveways integrating with existing systems.
