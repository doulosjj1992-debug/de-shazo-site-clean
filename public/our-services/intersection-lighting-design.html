Street Lighting Design

Effective street lighting is critical for visibility, security, and efficient traffic operations. We design systems that meet IES/TxDOT criteria, reduce glare, support multimodal travel, and optimize cost & energy performance.

45 Years of Proven Experience

Lighting for arterials, intersections, crosswalks, neighborhoods, campuses, and corridors across North Texas—balancing safety, standards, maintainability, and energy efficiency.

What We Do

  • Lighting Needs Assessment — context, pedestrian activity, approach lighting.
  • Photometric Modeling — illuminance/uniformity to IESNA, glare control.
  • Fixture Selection & Placement — poles, arms, optics, spacing, heights, foundations.
  • Signal & ITS Integration — mast arms, signage, traffic control coordination.
  • Energy & Sustainability — LED conversion, adaptive control, lifecycle analysis.
  • Compliance & Documentation — municipal, TxDOT, IES, ADA & MUTCD submittals.

Why It Matters

  • Improves safety & visibility at conflict points for all users.
  • Reduces glare with correct optics and uniformity.
  • Lowers energy & O&M costs via LED and smart controls.
  • Supports nighttime activity and reliable multimodal travel.

Example Projects

Private & Public Sector Experience

  • School & campus crosswalks
  • Neighborhood and collector corridors
  • Commercial / mixed-use districts
  • Trails and shared-use path crossings
