City of Pflugerville Comprehensive Plan

Master Thoroughfare Plan

Project Overview

DeShazo Group was selected as part of a multidisciplinary team by the City of Pflugerville to help update its Comprehensive Plan — the city's primary guide for planning, development regulation, and future infrastructure investment. DeShazo's role centered on shaping the transportation vision through thorough analysis, policy recommendations, and roadway planning.

📍Location:

City of Pflugerville, Texas

🎯Scope:

Comprehensive Plan Update including City Limits and ETJ

🚗Focus Area:

Transportation Element & Master Thoroughfare Plan

PFLUGERVILLE
💎 DeShazo's Contributions 🤝 Collaborative Process

What We Delivered

  1. Development of the Master Thoroughfare Plan
  2. Creation of recommended roadway cross-sections and basic design standards
  3. Formulation of transportation policies to support long-term planning

How We Worked Together

  1. Coordinated with the City Planning Department, Pflugerville Development Corporation, and other agencies
  2. Conducted community outreach meetings and stakeholder interviews
  3. Assessed existing conditions and projected future mobility needs

Strategic Recommendations

🎯 Future-Focused Planning & Design

  • Classify and prioritize transportation facilities to support anticipated growth
  • Introduce design standards that integrate walkability, safety, and community character

🏗️ Policy & Infrastructure Integration

  • Update access-management practices and street policies
  • Phase transportation improvements based on growth forecasts and funding strategies
