City of Crandall - Transportation Master Plan

Project Overview

DeShazo Group supported the City of Crandall in updating the Transportation Element of its Comprehensive Plan. The team developed a Master Thoroughfare Plan aligned with future land use and growth patterns, while incorporating community feedback and regional planning insights. This plan equips Crandall with a strategic roadmap for sustainable infrastructure investment and long-term mobility.
